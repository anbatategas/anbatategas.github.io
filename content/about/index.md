<h2 class="main-title" style="margin-top: 1rem; font-size: 2rem;">Introduction</h2>

I am a final-year undergraduate student at the School of Applied Mathematical and Physical Sciences at the National Technical University of Athens (NTUA). My academic journey is driven by a deep fascination with the fundamental laws of nature and the elegant mathematical frameworks that describe them.

My research interests are situated at the intersection of theoretical physics and computational modeling. On the theoretical and mathematical side, my focus lies in General Relativity, the Standard Model, and Quantum Field Theory. Complementing this, I leverage numerical methods to explore complex systems, with specific interests in Lattice Field Theories and Computational Statistical Mechanics.

I am deeply passionate about bridging the gap between abstract theoretical concepts and rigorous computational simulations, and I am actively seeking opportunities to further my research in these advanced physical domains.
