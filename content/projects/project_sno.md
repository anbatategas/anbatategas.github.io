This project provides a comprehensive analytical review of the Sudbury Neutrino Observatory (SNO), detailing the experimental and detection mechanisms that definitively resolved the Solar Neutrino Problem. Conducted as a term paper for the "Detector & Accelerator Technology" course at NTUA, the report evaluates how SNO successfully measured both the total and electron-specific solar neutrino fluxes independently of the Standard Solar Model (SSM).

The theoretical analysis and hardware evaluation developed in this study cover:
*   **Neutrino Oscillation & the MSW Effect:** Mathematical detailing of PMNS matrix flavor transformations and the Mikheyev-Smirnov-Wolfenstein (MSW) effect, which explains how electron neutrinos ($v_e$) change flavor due to elastic scattering interactions while propagating through dense solar matter.
*   **Heavy Water Cherenkov Detection:** Evaluation of the detector infrastructure, comprising a 1000-ton ultra-pure heavy water ($\mathrm{D_2O}$) target housed in a 12m diameter acrylic vessel. The target is surrounded by 9438 inward-facing Photomultiplier Tubes (PMTs) designed to capture Cherenkov radiation from electrons exceeding the local phase velocity of light.
*   **Statistical Cross-Section Separation:** Differentiation of the three primary neutrino reaction channels: Charged-Current (CC) for exclusive $v_e$ measurement, Neutral-Current (NC) for flavor-independent total flux measurement, and Elastic Scattering (ES) for highly directional solar tracking.
*   **Experimental Phasing Strategies:** Analysis of the observatory's three operational phases, which sequentially utilized pure $\mathrm{D_2O}$, the addition of ultra-pure $\mathrm{NaCl}$ to enhance neutron capture for the NC reaction, and the deployment of $\mathrm{^3He}$ proportional counters (NCDs) for distinct NC event tracking.
*   **Resolution of the Solar Neutrino Problem:** Review of the empirical data demonstrating that the total solar neutrino flux (derived from NC events) matches the predictions of the SSM, while the CC/NC ratio of approximately 30% definitively proved $v_e$ flavor transformation.
